* Estimates from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Everyone’s needs differ, so treat these as a starting point and adjust from your weekly weigh-ins.

How these numbers are worked out
  1. BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor): 10 × weight in kg + 6.25 × height in cm − 5 × age, then +5 for men or −161 for women.
  2. Maintenance (TDEE) = BMR × your activity factor, from 1.2 to 1.9.
  3. Goal: fat loss eats 20% below maintenance, muscle gain 10% above. Fat loss never goes below 1,500 kcal for men or 1,200 kcal for women.
  4. Protein: 2.0 g per kg for fat loss, 1.6 g for maintenance, 1.8 g for muscle gain. Above a BMI of 27 it uses the weight that gives a BMI of 27 at your height, so the target stays realistic.
  5. Fat: 25% of calories (30% for maintenance), and never under 0.6 g per kg.
  6. Carbs: whatever calories are left after protein and fat.

For healthy adults aged 16 and over. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or managing a medical condition, check with a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet.
